I've fixed a poor solder connection that resulted in intermediate disconnects on the E0 channel of the Pl-Ir wire to PCB. After this, 8 channel VNA measurements gave the scaling factors that we'll need to use to correct input powers for RF injection/rectification. I measured around 28-33db loss on the primary path input to electrode for all 4 channels. The cross loss was around -40db, so 10dB isolation between ports. I then wanted to test this on an actual RF injection station and measured 2.6Vpp on a scope at the end of the dicos and then 94mVpp when plugging the dico output into the boards and measuring the voltage output of the board. This came out to a 28.84db loss which was within 0.1db of VNA measurement. I then checked cross talk on other channels and measured the highest voltage at around 30mV. This 1/3 cross channel voltage coupling should be manageable and decently conditioned to correct for injection voltages.
