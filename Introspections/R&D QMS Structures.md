Medtronic has a QMS system that is too constrained. So much so that it is hindering advancement and which also hinders modification, forcing the organization to maintain these non-optimal practices. However, there is quite a few points of merit that this system has. I will outline here the high level structures that I believe would improve the typically "lose" data practices common in academia.

1) Design planning - write down what you say you want to do.
2) Equipment qualification - ensure that a measurement that you are making is accurate
3) Structured data storage
	1) ELN for raw data
	2) DHF for project specific measurement reporting
	3) RP for research projects
	4) TM for translated research projects
4) Full BOM of your product
