pTax: An end-to-end toolbox for accelerated pTx optimization for MRI
1) Overview: pTx is a critical tool for UHF MRI to generate quality images. However, depending on imaging sequences, different complexities of pTx optimizations are required to achieve acceptable image qualities. These different methods could be something as simple as static RF shimming where the magnitude and phase of the transmit channels are optimized and then fixed for an entire sequence, ranging to interleaved modes, dynamically applied RF shims as in DSC, gradient included optimizations like those found in kt-points or spokes, all the way up to un-parameterized optimization where RF phase and magnitude of every channel is updated during every RF waveform rastering step. We built the pTax toolbox to be expandable to handle all of these different pTx scenarios. Furthermore, this entire toolbox was written using the JAX python library to enable hardware agnostic and highly accelerated optimizations, thereby enabling real-time patient specific optimization on the scanner, even for large vector optimizations present in the RF waveform modifiable pTx scenarios.
